Turn your website into a true lead-generation engine. In this hands-on workshop you combine AI with your existing Keller Williams agent search platform to build a customized, modern site instead of relying on a cookie-cutter template.

## What you'll learn

- **Seamless KW agent site integration:** link your KW IDX search into a custom front-end site so visitors can search live listings while your personal brand stays front and center.
- **Prompt engineering for site design:** write custom AI prompts to build a minimalist, professional layout with custom color palettes, modern typography, hero sections and clear call-to-action buttons.
- **Dynamic lead magnets:** create gated Home Buying and Home Selling guides that capture names, contact details and client timelines before unlocking the content.
- **Your brand story:** use AI to polish and feature your bio, headshots, market expertise and achievement badges to build trust right away.
- **Real estate compliance:** embed the required legal disclaimers, including local brokerage information and the state notices (such as IABS and the Consumer Protection Notice).

## What you need to follow along

1. Your KW Agent Site URL
2. Your agent bio and headshot
3. Your brokerage office details and license number
4. Links to your Buyer and Seller guides or PDFs
5. Links to your required real estate disclosures (IABS and Consumer Protection Notice)

## Quick guide

1. **Create your Lovable account** with the invite link above.
2. **Gather your materials** (see "What you need to follow along" above), plus your logo and the KW Memorial logo.
3. **Copy the master prompt** below with the Copy button.
4. **Fill in every bracket.** Replace each `[Insert Your Preference Here]` with your own details: your name, 2 main colors plus 1 accent color, headline, bio, location, guide titles and descriptions, contact details and license number.
5. **Paste it into Lovable and submit.** Lovable builds the first version of your site.
6. **Add your files.** Upload your headshot, logos and guide PDFs, or ask Lovable to build a guide for you.
7. **Test the lead forms.** Make sure the Search Homes button, the guide pop-up and the contact form all work, and that each form asks for contact details before sending visitors to your search page.
8. **Finish the legal items.** Add your license number, the Equal Housing logo, your IABS and the Texas Real Estate Commission Consumer Protection Notice before you publish.

> Before you publish, scan the site for any leftover square brackets. Every placeholder should be replaced with your real details.
