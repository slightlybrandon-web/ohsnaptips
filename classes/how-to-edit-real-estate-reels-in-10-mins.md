## What you'll learn

- **The 10-minute workflow:** import clips in tour order, trim, add a hook, add feature text, caption, mix audio, pick a cover and post.
- **The 1-to-2 second cut rule:** keep each clip to about 2-3 seconds and cut on movement for a 12-15 second Reel.
- **The safe zone:** keep all text in the middle band so Instagram's buttons and caption never cover it.
- **Captions and audio:** auto-caption, proofread the prices and street names, keep music at 10-15% and record a quick voiceover.
- **Templates:** reuse a Reel layout you like and swap in your own clips in about a minute.
- **Posting for leads:** a caption template, a pinned comment, a Story poll and what to check at 24 hours.

## Before you start

1. Update Instagram.
2. In Instagram go to your profile photo, then the menu, then **Settings and privacy**, then **Media quality**, and turn on **Upload at highest quality**.
3. Save the 5 portrait practice clips to your camera roll and confirm they play.
4. Turn on Do Not Disturb and find a quiet spot for the voiceover.

> Menu names can move slightly between Instagram updates. If a button isn't where this guide says, look for the same words nearby. The steps and the order don't change.

## Step by step

1. **Start the Reel and import your clips.** Tap **+**, then **Reel**, then pick your clips in tour order: Front Entrance, Living Room, Kitchen, Staircase, Twilight Pool. Check that each subject sits in the middle of the frame. If you use a landscape photo, pinch to fill the screen and drag to re-center it instead of accepting the default crop.
2. **Trim it tight.** Tap **Edit video** and drag each clip's end handle until it runs about 2-3 seconds. Cut while the camera is moving. The first and last clips can run a touch longer.
3. **Add the hook and feature text.** Tap **Aa** and type a hook under 7 words, like "Wait for the twilight pool shot...". Show it for the first 2 seconds only. Add one 3-5 word tag per clip and time each one to its own clip.
4. **Add captions.** Tap the Sticker icon, then **Captions**. Proofread every line, because auto-captions often miss prices, street names and words like "quartz."
5. **Mix the music and a voiceover.** Add a track and set it to 10-15%. If a song is greyed out, that's a business-account licensing limit, so pick another or use a voiceover only. Hold the phone about 6 inches from your mouth to record.
6. **Use a template next time.** On a Reel you like, look for **Use Template** above the username, tap it and swap your clips into the slots. Save inspiring Reels to a collection called "Listing Inspiration."
7. **Choose a cover and post.** Pick your best frame, such as the twilight exterior, and check the square profile-grid crop so nothing is cut off. Add a caption, a location tag and 3-5 hashtags.
8. **Work the first hour.** Pin a comment with your call to action, share to Stories with a poll, reply to every comment and check Insights at 24 hours.

> **Before you post any listing:** confirm the price and facts, get the seller's permission, disclose any AI-generated or virtually staged images, and describe the property rather than a type of buyer or neighborhood. When in doubt, check with your broker first.

## Practice checklist

- 4-5 portrait clips imported in tour order
- Each trimmed to about 2-3 seconds (12-15 seconds total)
- Hook on the first clip, feature text on the Kitchen and Staircase clips
- All text inside the safe zone
- Music at 10-15%, captions on and proofread
- Cover chosen, grid crop checked, then shown to a partner or saved as a draft

## 7-day challenge

Post one listing Reel this week from a saved template, then check watch time, shares and saves at 24 hours. Those matter more than likes.
