This is a previously recorded live training.

Unlock your free KW Canva Enterprise account in this hands-on workshop. Follow along with your laptop and KW login to build launch-ready marketing assets right away.

## What you'll learn

- **MLS listing syncing:** connect your MLS listings straight into Canva to quickly fill your designs with property details and photos.
- **Agent brand kit setup:** standardize your headshots, brand colors, logos and typography for consistent marketing on every channel.
- **AI design tools:** use time-saving features like **Magic Switch** and **Background Remover** to transform graphics instantly.
- **KW Command integration:** export your finished Canva designs straight into KW Command for your campaigns and workflows.

## What you need

1. Your laptop
2. Your KW login credentials

## Quick guide

1. **Log in the right way.** Go to **canva.kw.com** (not canva.com). You'll know you're in the KW account by the red banner at the top. Your KW Canva login email, shown at the bottom left, is the one you use on your phone or tablet, with your Command password.
2. **Set up your brand kit.** Open **Brand** and create your own kit: logos (add the KW logos too), brand colors, fonts, your brand voice, and photos such as your headshot. After this, one click puts your logo, colors and fonts on any design.
3. **Make a design with Canva AI.** Describe what you want, like "open house flyer for a luxury listing in Houston, Texas," and edit it by chatting with the AI, for example "add dates and times."
4. **Resize for any channel.** Use **Resize** to turn a flyer into an Instagram post or a video. Choose **Copy & resize** to keep the original too, then nudge anything that moved.
5. **Brand it.** Select a design, open **Brand** and use **Shuffle** or the color editor to swap in your colors. Drag your logo in from the brand kit, and resize from the corners only so it keeps its shape.
6. **Use Background Remover.** Click your photo, choose **BG remover** in the toolbar, then place yourself on the design.
7. **Sync your listing from KW.** Open a KW branded template and click **Use this brand template**. In **Apps**, search for **Keller Williams** (not Keller Home Loans), choose **Connect** and sign in to Command. Use **All listings** to find any property by address or MLS number.
8. **Drag listing photos and details onto the flyer.** Drop each photo on its frame and double-click to zoom. Drop the address and price onto the text to replace it. Type the bedrooms and bathrooms yourself if a number doesn't land correctly, and delete any leftover placeholder text such as "KWDBA."
9. **Add a QR code.** Search **QR code** in **Apps**, paste your link (such as an open house sign-in page) and generate it right on the flyer.
10. **Download.** Choose **Share**, then **Download**. Use PDF for printing, JPEG or PNG for online, and MP4 for video. Everything auto-saves, and you can group projects in folders.

> You can also make short videos in Canva. Pick a real estate video template, swap in your handle, contact details and photos, and replace the music by dragging a new track onto the audio.
