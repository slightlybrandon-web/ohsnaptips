Stop letting admin tasks run your day. In this masterclass you pair Gemini AI inside your KW Workspace with your Google Business Profile to turn your local online presence into a lead-generation and time-saving machine.

## What you'll learn

- **Mobile geotagged photo boosts:** upload smartphone photos straight to Google Maps to help your local search rankings.
- **High-converting posts:** create Event and Offer posts that turn local searchers into booked appointments.
- **Gemini AI prompts:** generate localized profile updates and personalized 5-star review replies in seconds.
- **Direct booking and lead intake:** link Google Appointment Schedules to your profile and attach Google Forms for automated intake.
- **Mobile Drive camera tricks:** scan paper documents into text-searchable PDFs and find them instantly with OCR search.

## Before you start

Your Google Business Profile must already be created and active. If it isn't, follow our guide first: [How to Create a Google Business Profile](/guide/how-to-create-a-google-business-profile/).

## Quick guide

1. **Add your KW account as a manager.** On your Google Business Profile, open the three-dot menu, then **Google business settings**, then **People and access**, then **Add**. Keep your own Gmail as the primary owner so you always have full control, and give helpers access here instead of sharing your Google login.
2. **Post geotagged photos from your phone.** Open the Google Maps app, tap **Business**, then **Add photo**. Keep location services on, and your phone tags the place for you. Post local spots, not just listings, to show Google you're active in your area.
3. **Write a post with Gemini.** Paste a prompt like the one below into Gemini, fill in your neighborhood, and ask it to expand to about 1,500 characters in paragraphs.
4. **Add a flyer and publish.** Make a quick graphic in Canva, then in your profile choose **Add post**, upload the image and paste your text. You can switch the post type to **Event** (add the date and time) or **Offer**, and add a **Learn more** button that links to your site or valuation page. New posts can take a few minutes to appear.
5. **Ask for reviews a little at a time.** Add a review request line to your Gmail signature (Gemini can write a few options), and save your review link as a QR code in a photo album on your phone to show at a closing. Don't ask all your clients at once, because Google may flag the reviews.
6. **Save a Gmail template for review requests.** In Gmail go to **Settings**, then **See all settings**, then **Advanced**, and turn on **Templates**. Write the email once, then use the three-dot menu, then **Templates**, then **Save draft as template**.
7. **Reply to every review.** Use the second prompt below and mention the city so Google learns where you work.
8. **Add a booking link.** In Google Calendar choose **Create**, then **Appointment schedule**. Set your hours, limit how far ahead people can book (14 days in the class) and set a minimum notice (4 hours). Add a Google Form as a client questionnaire and put its link in the description, then paste your booking link into the **Booking calendar** section of your profile.
9. **Scan paperwork with Google Drive.** In the Drive app tap **+**, then **Scan**, adjust the corners, pick a filter and upload. Each scan becomes a searchable PDF, so you can find it later by searching words inside it.

Prompt for step 3:

```
Write a three sentence Google business update announcing a recent home sale in [neighborhood, city]. Highlight local amenities like nearby parks and schools and end with a call to action asking homeowners to check their home value.
```

Prompt for step 7:

```
Draft a warm two sentence response to a five-star Google review from [client name] who bought a home in [city]. Mention local market knowledge and express gratitude.
```

> If you want your reviews to also show on HAR, your profile needs a real service address. Google may ask you to verify it by text or with a short video of you at the office.
