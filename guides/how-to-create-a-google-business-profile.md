An optimized Google Business Profile (also called Google My Business) is one of the best free ways to get found locally, collect customer reviews and show up on Google Maps. This guide follows the video from start to finish.

## Create the profile

1. Go to **google.com/business**.
2. Sign in with the Google account you want tied to the profile.
3. Enter your **business name** and click **Create a Business** if it doesn't show up.
4. Choose the **business category** that best describes what you do.
5. Enter your **location details**, such as your address or the area you serve.
6. Add a **phone number** and your **website**, if you have one.

## Get verified

Google needs to confirm the business is yours before the profile goes live. Choose a verification method when Google offers it and enter the code you receive. Verifying quickly gets you showing up in Search and Maps sooner.

## Optimize your listing

- Pick the right category, and keep your name, address and phone number accurate.
- Add your hours, a short and clear description, and good photos.
- Ask happy customers for reviews.
- Post updates regularly to stay active.

> The more complete your profile is, and the more often you update it, the better your chances of attracting local clients.
