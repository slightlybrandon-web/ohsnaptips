Connect your e-signature apps to Command so contracts go out digitally and every file stays in one place.

## Connect your accounts

1. Move your mouse to your name in the top right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Open **Application Integrations** and scroll down to **Dotloop** and/or **DocuSign**.
3. Click **Connect Account** for the one you want.
4. Choose **Login Here**, unless you haven't created an account yet. Follow the steps to sign in with the login details from the paperwork emailed to you by UpTech Pro.
5. For Dotloop, sign in with your **KW credentials**. You must use your KW account to log in.

Once you are connected, you are ready to learn how to use Opportunities.

## Can't find Dotloop or DocuSign in Command?

1. Go to **Connect More Apps** and search for the app in the KW Marketplace search bar.
2. Choose **Dotloop**, select **View Details** and click **Buy Now**.
3. During checkout you will see Dotloop is **no charge**. Click **Continue**.
4. Check the box agreeing to the terms and conditions and click **Place Order**.
5. Close the tab, return to Command and **refresh** your browser.
6. Connect your Dotloop or DocuSign account.
