Done with an app you added from the KW Marketplace? Here is how to remove it by cancelling the subscription.

## Steps

1. Go to **agent.kw.com** and log in.
2. Move your mouse to the top right corner of the screen and click the **Marketplace** icon, right next to your name.
3. Click the **My Apps** tab.
4. Hover over the app you want to remove and click the little **gear icon** in its top right corner.
5. Select **Manage App**.
6. Choose **Cancel Subscription**.

> That's it. Three simple moves: open My Apps, click the gear, cancel the subscription.
