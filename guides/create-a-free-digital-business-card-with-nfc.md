A digital business card is always in your pocket and easy to share. Here is how to make one for free with the HiHello app and send it with a tap.

## Create your card

1. Search for **HiHello** in the App Store or Google Play and download it.
2. Open the app and tap the **plus** button in the top right corner to create a new card.
3. Fill in what you want to share: first and last name, occupation, phone number, address, email and your social accounts.
4. Tap **Save** in the top right corner.
5. Go back into the card any time to add or change your photo.

> HiHello also stores and saves contacts, and can send your card to your contacts.

## Share it with NFC

An NFC tag lets people get your card with a single tap. The one in the video is an NFC bracelet that cost about five dollars on Amazon.

1. In HiHello, choose the business card you want to use.
2. Tap the **Write to NFC** button on the right.
3. Hold your bracelet or tag near your phone's NFC area until it writes.
4. Now tap it against any smart device and they receive your contact card with all of your info.
