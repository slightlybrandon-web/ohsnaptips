Google Calendar can give you a free booking page. Clients pick an open time and it lands on your calendar automatically. Here is how to set it up, setting by setting.

## Start the schedule

1. Go to **calendar.google.com**.
2. Click **Create** at the top left, then choose **Appointment schedule**.
3. Give it a title and choose how long each appointment lasts: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or a custom length.

## Set your availability

1. Choose the days and hours you are open. The default is standard business hours, and you can add weekends.
2. To repeat a time across other days, click **Copy times to all selected**.
3. Block any days or times you do not want booked.

> Tip: if you do not block your calendar, you are always available to clients.

## Set your scheduling rules

1. **Scheduling window:** skip the 60-day default. About 2 weeks keeps clients committed to the time they book.
2. **Minimum notice:** add a buffer, like 4 hours, so nobody books you right before the start time.
3. **Buffer between appointments:** I recommend at least 30 minutes so you can prepare.
4. **Maximum bookings per day:** cap it if you want, or leave it open.
5. **Allow guests to invite others:** useful when a client wants to bring a partner or an assistant.

## Choose your calendar and location

1. **Calendars:** the free version connects one calendar per schedule, so use one calendar for everything.
2. Leave **Booking page and photo name** as it is.
3. **Location:** pick an in-person address, **Google Meet** (built in, and my recommendation), a phone call, or none for now.

## Add a description and booking form

1. Write a short **description** about what clients should prepare. It shows on the calendar event and in their confirmation email.
2. In the **booking form**, click **Add item** to ask for more than name and email. Add a phone number and make it required.

> Note: payments and cancellation policies need a paid Google Workspace plan, so they are not in the free version.

## Save and share your link

1. Click **Save**. A bar appears across your calendar on the days you are available.
2. Click the bar and choose **Copy link**.
3. Send the link to clients, or add it to your website or email signature.

## What your client sees

Your client opens the link and sees the appointment name, who it is with and the open times. They pick a time, enter their details and phone number, and click **Book**. They get a confirmation email, and the appointment appears on your calendar.
