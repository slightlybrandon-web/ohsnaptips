Google Calendar can give you a free booking page. Clients pick an open time, and it lands on your calendar automatically. Share the link by text or email, add it to your website, or put it in your email signature.

## Create the appointment schedule

1. Open **Google Calendar** on your computer.
2. Click **Create**, then choose **Appointment schedule**.
3. Give it a clear title, like "30-Minute Consultation."

## Set your availability

1. Choose how long each appointment lasts.
2. Pick the days and hours you are open for booking.
3. Set how far ahead clients can book.

> Tip: Only offer the hours you actually want to work. Clients can only book inside the windows you set.

## Add buffers, limits and a confirmation message

1. Add a **buffer** before or after each appointment so you are never back to back.
2. Set a **maximum number of bookings per day**.
3. Write a short **confirmation message** with anything the client should know, like where to meet or what to bring.

## Share your booking page

1. Save the schedule.
2. Click **Share** and copy the **booking page link**.
3. Send it to clients, or paste it into your website, social bio or email signature.

## Test it as a client

Open your booking link in a private (incognito) window and book a time yourself. Check that the appointment shows up on your calendar and that the confirmation looks right. Then delete the test booking.
