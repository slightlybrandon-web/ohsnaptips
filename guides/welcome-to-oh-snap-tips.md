This is a sample guide so you can see how everything looks. Delete it from the admin page once you have added your own.

## How guides work

1. Go to **ohsnaptips.com/admin** and sign in.
2. Click **New Guide** and paste your YouTube link.
3. Write your numbered steps and click **Save**.

> Tip: leave the YouTube field blank for a written-only article.
