Make it easy for clients to get your information by putting a QR code right on your phone. These tips work on Apple and Android.

## Step 1: Make your QR code

1. Go to **qrcode-monkey.com**.
2. Click the **vCard** option and enter the information you want clients to receive.
3. Scroll to the bottom and click **Create QR Code**.
4. Download the **PNG** and wait for it to finish.

If you already use HiHello, you can use its QR code and widget instead.

## Option A: Use it as your lock screen wallpaper

1. Save the downloaded file to your **Photos** album.
2. Open **Photos**, select the QR code and take a **screenshot** of it. This makes sure it fits the whole screen and doesn't get resized.
3. Set that screenshot as your **wallpaper**.

## Option B: Put it on your Today View (iPhone)

Prefer to keep your own wallpaper on the lock screen? Use a widget instead.

1. Open the App Store and download the **Photo Widget** app.
2. In the app, tap **Widget** at the bottom, then **Photo** at the top.
3. Add a new album, pick your QR code, tap **Done** and rename the album (for example "QR Code") so it is easy to find.
4. Go to your Today View, scroll to the bottom and tap **Edit**, then the **plus** button in the top left.
5. Find **Photo Widget** and choose the **big** size so it is easy to scan.
6. Move it where you want it. Double tap the widget and choose the **QR Code** album.

Your QR code is now scannable from your lock screen or home screen with a swipe.
