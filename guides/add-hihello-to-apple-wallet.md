Share your digital business card faster by adding your HiHello card to Apple Wallet. This takes about 30 seconds.

## Add your card to Wallet

1. Swipe down on your Home Screen, search for **HiHello** and open it.
2. Find the card you want to share.
3. Go to the **Share** option and tap it. You'll see a few different options.
4. **Scroll up** until you see **Add to Apple Wallet**.
5. Tap **Add to Apple Wallet**, then select **Add** to put it in your wallet.

## Share it

1. **Double-click** to open your Wallet.
2. Find your **HiHello card** inside.
3. Show it to the person you're meeting. They simply scan it.
