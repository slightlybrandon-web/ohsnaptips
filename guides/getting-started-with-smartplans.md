SmartPlans automate your follow-up so you can save time. Here is how to download, edit, assign and automate them.

## Add SmartPlans from the library

1. Open the **SmartPlans** applet. Find your own plans or click the **Library** tab.
2. Search for what you need and click **Add Smart Plan**. Examples:
   - **New to Keller Williams** (new licensed or experienced agent). It prompts you to download **My Quarterly Call Plan**. Click **Download**, then **Download** again.
   - **Open House Follow-Up**, made by Keller Williams. It also prompts you to download **My Monthly Neighborhood Nurture** and **My Long Term Nurture**.
   - **Twilio:** if you set up a Twilio account, download **New Twilio Introduction to Existing Contact** to tell your clients about your new number.
3. Hover over the text or click **View Steps** to see what each step does.

## Edit a SmartPlan

Click **My Smart Plans**, then the **pencil** icon to edit. Every plan is fully editable.

1. **Proofread** everything you download so it matches your brand and personality.
2. Blue fields are **merge fields**. For example, the contact field swaps in the client's first name.
3. Replace the agent field with **your own name** so your signature looks the way you want.

The "New to Keller Williams" plan sends an email, waits about three days, texts the client on day four, waits again, then tasks you to make a call (with a mini script you can follow). Last, it adds the client to **My Quarterly Call Plan**, which tasks you every 90 days to check in.

## Assign contacts

1. Click **Add Contact** in the top right.
2. Search your database, or type a **tag** and filter by it. Select all, then click **Add to Smart Plan**.
3. Start them all on day one, or schedule a start date.
4. When you add many contacts, **stagger them** so only a few get your message each day.
5. Click **Continue**.

## Unsubscribe contacts

1. Open the plan and click **Add Contact**.
2. Contacts already in the plan show on the right. Select them, click **Unsubscribe All** and confirm.

> Emails that already went out do not come back.

## Automate with trigger events

1. Open a plan, for example **My Open House Follow-Up**.
2. Add a **Trigger Event**: any contact with the **Open House** tag is automatically added to the plan. You can add multiple trigger events for different tags.
3. Click **Save**.

The open house plan runs 21 days, with 15 steps and 7 touches, so you don't have to do all of the reaching out yourself.

## Tasks

When a plan includes a task, you get a notification in the Command app and the desktop version. Find all of your tasks by clicking the **Tasks** icon just above SmartPlans.
