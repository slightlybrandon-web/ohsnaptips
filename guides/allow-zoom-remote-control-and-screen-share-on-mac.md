If Zoom can't share your screen or accept remote control on your Mac, it needs permission in System Preferences. Here is how to turn both on.

## Steps

1. Open **System Preferences** and go to **Security & Privacy**, then the **Privacy** tab.
2. Click the **padlock** at the bottom left and unlock it with your fingerprint or computer password.
3. Select **Accessibility** in the left column, find **Zoom** in the list and check the box.
4. Go back to the left column, scroll down and select **Screen Recording**.
5. Check the box next to **Zoom**.
6. If Zoom is open, your Mac will ask to quit and reopen it. Choose **Quit & Reopen**.
7. Click the **padlock** again to lock your settings and close System Preferences.

## Try it

Start or join a Zoom meeting. You can now share your screen and let others control it.

> On newer versions of macOS the screen is called **System Settings**, and the options live under **Privacy & Security**. The steps are the same.
