Google gives businesses free marketing materials, and they are great if you already have Google reviews.

## Steps

1. Go to **marketingkit.withgoogle.com**.
2. Type your business name in the search bar and select it when it pops up.
3. Once your business is verified, scroll to the bottom and click **Customize**.
4. Choose a favorite review to feature.
5. Click **Next** and pick your **material style and color**. There are many options, from art styles to sign scripts with several color choices to match your brand, plus a chalkboard style.
6. Click **Next**, then **Download**. It saves to your computer.
7. Open the downloaded file. You get folders of graphics ready to post on your social platform of choice.
