A **Gem** is a custom version of Gemini that you set up once and reuse. Instead of retyping the same background and rules at the start of every chat, you save them in the Gem. It is Google's version of a custom GPT or a Claude Project.

This guide builds a **Social Media Strategy Gem**, but the steps are the same for any Gem.

## Open Gems

1. Click the **Google apps grid** (the little waffle) and choose **Gemini**.
2. If you do not see Gems, click the **Settings** icon at the bottom of the left column and turn on **Gems**.
3. Open **Gems**. This is your custom Gem page.

> **Heads up:** Google is renaming Gems to **Skills**. They work the same way, so the steps below still apply.

## Create your Gem

1. Click **New Gem**.
2. Enter a **Name**, such as *Social Media Strategy Gem*.
3. Add a short **Description** of what the Gem is for.
4. In the **Instructions** box, paste your prompt: who the Gem should act as, what to use as its source (for example, a brand profile you upload), the format you want back, and the rules it must follow every time.
5. Click **Save** at the top.

A good prompt covers three things:

- **Source:** what to base answers on, such as your brand voice and audience.
- **Format:** the layout you want, such as a four-week plan in a table.
- **Rules:** what to always do, so every run comes out the same way.

## Use it

1. Click **Start chat** after saving.
2. Paste in your details. In the video, that was a made-up business.
3. Click **Go**. The Gem answers using your saved rules, with no setup typed again.

Then keep going in the same Gem with follow-ups like:

- "Build me a social media plan for November."
- "Write three captions for the holiday pie pre-orders."
- "Make me a TikTok plan."

## Find your Gem later

In the left column, open **Gems** (under **More** if you do not see it). Click your Gem to jump back in. Every chat you run inside it is saved there, and it follows your rules each time.

> **Tip:** Once the Gem works, add a few test prompts you use often, so you can check it still gives you what you expect.
