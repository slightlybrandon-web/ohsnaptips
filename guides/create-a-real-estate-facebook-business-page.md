Create a Facebook business page in about five minutes. You need a personal Facebook account to do it.

## Create the page

1. Log into your **personal Facebook** account.
2. On the left side, click the **grid icon** and open the **Create Page** tab in the second column on the right.
3. **Name your page.** Best practice for agents is first name, last name, then "Real Estate Agent", "Realtor" or "Real Estate". It's up to you.
4. Pick up to **three categories**, with at least one chosen. Good picks: **Real Estate Agent**, **Real Estate Services** and **Real Estate Agency**.
5. Write a **description**. Think elevator pitch: short, sweet and to the point, whatever grabs your audience fastest.
6. Click the blue **Create Page** button.

## Add your photos

1. Add a **profile photo** and a **cover photo** that pairs well with your brand. You can do this later if you don't have them yet.
2. Click **Save**.

## Add more details

1. Create a **username** that customers can easily remember. Using your name is a great idea.
2. Click **Edit Page Info** in the bottom left corner to add your **phone number, email, website, office information** and anything else important.

## Add your compliance information

Make sure your agent page is in compliance by adding your compliance documentation from **HAR**.

1. On **har.com**, go to your dashboard, then **My Account**, **Account Info**, then **Information About Brokerage Services**.
2. Copy your **IABS** text and paste it into your Facebook page settings. Copy the **link** and paste that too.
3. Do the same with your **Consumer Protection Notice (CPN)**: copy the text, paste it into the additional details, and add the link. The form auto-saves.
4. View the page as a different profile to confirm your compliance information shows right on the page.

Don't forget to like your page and invite your friends.
