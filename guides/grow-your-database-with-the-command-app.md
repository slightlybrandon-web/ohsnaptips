Add contacts to your Command database on the go with the Command app, available on the App Store and Google Play.

## Add a contact

1. Download the **Command** app, open it and log in with your Command credentials.
2. From the home screen you'll find the important things front and center.
3. To add a contact, tap the **Contacts** icon at the bottom of the screen and tap the **plus** button. You can also tap the **plus** button in the bottom right corner of the home screen and choose **Add Contact**.
4. Enter the client's information as you would on desktop. A **full name** is required. You can also add phone numbers, email addresses and the other fields you'd find on the desktop version.
5. Tap **Save** in the top right corner.

All of your client's contact information is now in one central place, in the palm of your hand.
