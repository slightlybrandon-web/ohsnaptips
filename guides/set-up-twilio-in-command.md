Twilio is a text messaging service that helps you leverage your thumbs, so you don't have to send repeated messages yourself.

## Subscribe to Twilio

1. Click your **name** in the top right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page. Twilio shows as not connected.
3. Click **Subscribe in Marketplace** under Twilio. This opens the Command Marketplace.
4. Click **Buy Now**, then **Buy Now** again in the top right.
5. Choose how many **credits** you want. Starting at **300** is recommended, and you can add more later. The discount is already applied, so no code is needed.
6. Click **Continue**, enter your billing information and click **Continue** again.
7. Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions and click **Place Order**. Then close the tab.

## Set up your Twilio number

1. Refresh the **Settings** tab and scroll back down to Twilio. You'll now see **Setup Number**.
2. Choose to **transfer an existing number** or **create a new one**.
3. Enter the **area code** you want and click **Search**.
4. Click **Claim Number** on the one you like, then **Yes, Claim It**. The number is yours.

## Set up call forwarding

1. Click **Manage**, then set up **Call Forwarding**. This sends any text reply back to your main line.
2. Enter your primary phone number. Forwarding is optional. You can always read messages in the Command app.
3. Click **Accept**, enter the **verification code** sent to that phone, and click **Verify**.

> Allow up to **72 hours** for the connection to be made.

Twilio is now tied to your Command account and your phone number.
