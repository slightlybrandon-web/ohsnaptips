Get all of your contacts out of your iPhone, Android, tablet or Samsung and into a clean, usable spreadsheet in a few taps, so you can upload them to a CRM like Command.

## Steps

1. Download **SA Contacts Lite** from the App Store or Google Play. The Lite version is free and is enough for this.
2. Open the app and choose whether to export **all contacts**, **select groups** or **single contacts**.
3. Choose your **property style**. We want a **CSV** file, so leave it on **separated** (comma-separated). There are other options if you need them.
4. Tap the **Start** button at the bottom.
5. When your contacts are ready, choose how to export them:
   - Save them to a **USB stick**.
   - Use the **Wi-Fi** option: type the URL shown into your computer's web browser to download a zip file.
   - **Email** them, if the file is small enough.
   - Tap **More** for other places to send the file.
6. Close the app when you're done.
