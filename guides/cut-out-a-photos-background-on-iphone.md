Turn a photo into a translucent cut-out in a few seconds, with no editing app. This works right inside the Photos app on your iPhone.

## Cut it out

1. Open **Photos** and select the photo you want.
2. Open the photo, then **touch and hold your finger on it** until the subject pops.
3. **Drag your finger out** to lift the subject away from the background.
4. Go to the very top of the screen and tap the **back button**, then let go.
5. Your new cut-out is now in the background, ready to use.

> You can use the cut-out as a PNG whenever you want, for example on a flyer, a social post or a business card.
