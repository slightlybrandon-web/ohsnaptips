Connect Facebook to Command so you can send out campaigns with little effort.

## Steps

1. Go to **agent.kw.com** and log in with your KW account.
2. Click your **name** in the top right and choose **Settings**.
3. Find the **two Facebook tabs**. This is where you connect your accounts.

### Facebook post scheduling

1. Click **Connect Account**.
2. Choose the **business page** you want to connect to Command and click **Connect**.

### Ads Manager

1. Click **Connect Account**, open the drop-down and choose the page you want to connect.
2. You must agree to Facebook's **Lead Ad Policy** to connect. You'll be redirected automatically. Choose the business page you need to agree to terms for and click **Accept**.
3. Go back to Command, select the business page one more time and click **Done** in the bottom right corner.

Your Facebook page is now connected and ready to send campaigns through Command. Questions? Reach out to your local Market Center tech trainer.
