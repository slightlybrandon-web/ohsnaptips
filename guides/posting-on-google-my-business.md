Posting on your Google business profile lets you reach people right on the Google search page, and clients don't have to leave Google to see it.

## Create a post

1. Go to **google.com/business** and log into your business profile manager.
2. Select the business you want to manage.
3. Click **Create Post** in the center of the screen, or click **Posts** in the left column.
4. Click **Create First Post**. You can choose a COVID-19 update, an offer, **What's New**, an event or a product. This example uses **What's New**.
5. Upload a photo and write your copy.
6. Choose the button you want, for example **Learn More**.

> Tip: you can reuse your social content. Open a Facebook business post, click the **three dots** and choose **Copy Link** (or copy the post URL from the address bar). Paste it as the button link, or use your Facebook business page address instead.

## Check how it looks

1. Click **View as Search** and scroll down to your business card to see what a searcher will see.
2. Add as many posts as you want, as often as you want.

The more often you post, the more likely a client finds up-to-date information about what's going on with your business.
