If customers search online and don't find you, they find a competitor. A free Google Business Profile fixes that.

## Create the profile

1. Go to **google.com/business**.
2. Make sure you are signed in to the Gmail account you want tied to the profile.
3. Type your business name. If it doesn't appear, click **Create a Business**.
4. Add your **business category** and click **Next**.
5. Choose **Yes** if customers can visit a storefront or office, or **No** if not. If yes, enter the address. If Google suggests a different address, choose **None of these**.
6. If you offer deliveries or home and office visits, choose **Yes**, otherwise **No**.
7. Enter a **business phone number** you can keep handy, since you may need it to verify.
8. Add your **website**. If you don't have one, even a simple landing page helps.

## Get verified

Pick a method and enter the code you receive:

- **Phone or text**, if your number supports it.
- **Email.**
- **Postcard** mailed by the US Post Office with a verification code.

If a code isn't accepted, try a different method.

## Finish the profile

1. Add your **business hours**, or click **Skip** if you don't have any.
2. Choose whether to turn on **messaging** and **call history**. You can change these later.
3. Write a **business description**. It is your elevator pitch, so keep it intentional, straight and to the point.
4. Add **photos**: interior, exterior, your logo and anything you want to share. Everyone sees these, so avoid blurry photos and poor-quality video.
5. Claim your free advertising credit or skip it, then click **Continue** to see your page.

> The more information you add, and the more often you post, the faster you can move up Google search.

## Manage it later

- On a computer, click the **grid icon** in the top right and open the **Business Profile** applet. Select the business you want to manage. You can add more than one.
- On your phone, download the **Google My Business** app from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same Gmail account. It has the same features.

## Ask for reviews

1. In the app, tap the **Customers** icon at the bottom.
2. Tap **Ask for Reviews**.
3. Copy the link, text it, email it or share it on social.

Start asking. The only way to get to the top of Google is to work with Google.
