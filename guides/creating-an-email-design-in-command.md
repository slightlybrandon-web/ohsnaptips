Design newsletters, open house emails, listing emails and more with the Designs applet. This example builds a "Neighborhood Spotlight" newsletter.

## Start a design

1. Go to **agent.kw.com** and log into Command.
2. Open the **Designs** applet (the paper with a paintbrush).
3. Click **Create Design** in the top right and choose **Email**, then **Continue**.
4. Pick a template. Click **Load More** to see more. This example uses **Approachable Newsletter B**. Click **Next**.
5. Name your design (for example, "Neighborhood Spotlight") and start customizing.

## Customize it

1. **Headline:** click the text to rename it, highlight it, center it, choose **Heading 1** so it is nice and big, and click **Done**. You can delete any photo or content you don't need.
2. **Market snapshot:** drag the **Market Snap** widget from the right side into the email. Click the **pencil**, rename it, type a zip code, find the neighborhood and click **Done**.
3. **Copy:** click the pencil over the text, highlight it all, write your copy (a short promo for the neighborhood), center it and click **Done**.
4. **Spotlight a local business:** click the photo, choose **Edit**, then **Replace** and pick your image. Add a centered title and a teaser. Click the button, choose **Edit**, name it and paste the business's web address.
5. **Call to action:** replace the photo with one from a social post, rewrite the headline and body to match, and set the button to **Link to Web Address** with the URL of your Facebook post.

> Anything that looks broken or empty (name, contact info, footer) comes from your **Marketing Profile**, so there is no need to fix it here.

## Preview, test and save

1. Click **Options** in the top right and choose **Preview**.
2. Send a **test email** to yourself first. It arrives in about 10 minutes.
3. When you are happy, click **Save**, then the **X**, then **Save Changes** again.

You can download the template or make a copy to reuse it in different ways.
