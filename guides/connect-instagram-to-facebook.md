Connect your social channels so you can post and schedule from one central place.

## Connect the accounts

1. On your **Facebook business page**, scroll to the bottom of the left column and open **Settings**.
2. Select **Instagram** in the settings column, then **Connect Account**.
3. Choose whether Instagram messages go directly to your Facebook business page, then click **Continue**.
4. Log in to your Instagram account. Make sure it is your **business** Instagram account.

## Test it with a post

1. Go back to your Facebook business page and select **Manage** (or go to **business.facebook.com**). You'll see Instagram and Facebook tied together.
2. Click **Create Post**.
3. Choose your media. To share on Instagram, the post must be a **photo or video**.
4. Write your copy and make sure **both pages** are selected.
5. Optional: **schedule** the post for different times and days per channel, or **save it as a draft**.
6. Click **Publish** (or **Publish Now**).
7. Refresh your business page to see your post live.
