Here are the HAR courses you need to take to get your real estate business ready, and how to register for each one.

## Find the courses

1. Open a new browser tab, go to **har.com** and log in to your members-only portal.
2. In the blue column on the left, click **Training**.
3. Open the **Training** drop-down and choose **Real Estate Course Finder**.

## Register for each required course

For each one below: type the name in the keyword search box, click **Course Search**, choose **English** or **Spanish**, then pick an available time that fits your schedule.

1. **Matrix Overview, Part 1**
2. **Matrix Overview Part 2: Customization**
3. **CMA Price It Right (Matrix)**
4. **Orientation**

> Finish all four and you will be a real estate pro in no time.
