How to get your commission request submitted to your Market Center from the Opportunities applet in KW Command.

## Find your opportunity and sync the transaction

1. Open the **Opportunities** applet inside Command.
2. Go to **All Opportunities** and find the opportunity you need.
3. In the details, click the **pencil icon** next to **Property** to edit the property address.
4. Click **Save**, then **Sync Transaction**.

## Add an offer

1. Open the **Offers and Commissions** tab on the right side of the screen.
2. Click **Add a New Offer**, name it anything you like and click **Create Offer**.
3. Make sure your **offer date** and **close date** match.
4. Enter the buyer or seller name (whoever you are not representing) and that side's agent. Anything with a **red asterisk** is required.
5. Click **Terms** at the bottom right. Enter the cash down payment and the finance amount to get the sale price.
6. Add earnest money as a percentage or a fixed amount. Include an option fee and its termination days if one was agreed on. There are extra fields for seller costs.
7. Use **Agent Analysis** to write the pros and cons of the offer. It is handy when several offers come in.
8. Click **Done**.

> You can add multiple offers. Click the **three dots** on an offer to edit, reject or accept it.

## Manage the commission

1. After an offer is accepted, click **Manage Commission**.
2. Click **Edit General Information** to set the commission rate, sales price, total commission, contract date and close date. Total units must always equal **1**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

## Edit agent payments

1. Scroll to the bottom and click **Edit Agent Payment**.
2. Here you can give toward **KW Cares** or **Kids Can**, add scholarships, and enter bonuses, concessions, deductions, and inside or outside referrals.
3. To add a **concession**, choose Concession, enter the amount, turn on the first two toggles and click **Add**. Use **Add Item** for other payment types.

> If you give a concession, add a note in the summary saying you are giving the seller that amount.

### Outside referrals

1. Choose **Outside Referral** and enter the percentage or amount for your associate.
2. Enter the agent's **tax ID type** and number. You cannot enter all zeroes. Use the agent's EIN or Social Security number.
3. Checks should be written to a brokerage. If so, use the brokerage EIN and its company details.
4. Add the agent name, address, phone number and email if you have them, then click **Add** and **Save Changes**.

### Add another agent

Click **Add Another Agent** next to the payment option, search for the agent and enter their commission, any KW Cares amount and any other items. Click **Save Changes**.

## Submit

1. If you added an agent or changed any payment, open **Edit Agent Payment** and click **Calculate Commission**. Errors should clear when it refreshes.
2. Click **Submit**.
3. Decide whether to donate extra to KW Cares or Kids Can, then click **Skip** or **Continue**, and then **Submit**.

Once your request is approved, find your **DA** in the **Documents** tab, in the **Closed** folder. The DA appears no more than five days before closing. Questions? Reach out to your Market Center.
