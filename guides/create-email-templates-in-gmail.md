Answering the same questions again and again? Save that email as a Gmail template once, then drop it into any new message in seconds. This works in a regular Gmail account, so you do not need a paid plan.

## Turn on Templates

Templates are off by default, so switch them on first.

1. Click the **gear icon** in the top right corner of Gmail, then choose **Settings**.
2. Click **See all settings**.
3. Open the **Advanced** tab (it is on the far right of the settings tabs).
4. Find **Templates** and change it from **Disabled** to **Enabled**.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

## Write your template email

1. Click **Compose** to start a new message. Leave the **To** field blank, since the recipient does not matter yet.
2. Add a **subject line**. This becomes the **template's name**, so make it something you will recognize. You can change the name when you save.
3. Write your full email. Put anything you will change each time in **[brackets]**, such as [NAME], [DATE] or [YOUR PHONE OR WEBSITE].

> **Tip:** Brackets are your reminder of what to update before you send. Gmail does not fill them in for you, so look for them every time you use the template.

## Save it as a template

Do not hit Send. Save it instead.

1. At the bottom of the message, click the **three dots** (**More options**).
2. Choose **Templates**, then **Save draft as template**.
3. Click **Save as new template**.
4. Keep the name or change it, then click **Save**.

## Use your template

Say you need to send that same answer again, and the original email is long gone. That is what the template is for.

1. Click **Compose**.
2. Click the **three dots**, then choose **Templates**.
3. Pick your template from the list. It fills in the whole email.
4. Add the **recipient** and replace everything in **[brackets]**.
5. Click **Send**, or use **Schedule send** to send it later.

## Good templates to save

- Replies to new inquiries and frequently asked questions
- Welcome and onboarding emails for new clients
- Quotes, follow-ups and thank-you notes
- Appointment confirmations and reminders

Save the ones you write most often, and you will get back hours every week.
