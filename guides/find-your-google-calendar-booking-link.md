Set up a booking page but can't remember where the link went? You can pull it up again in a few clicks.

## Find your link

1. Open **Google Calendar** on your computer.
2. Click your **appointment schedule** on the calendar.
3. Click **Open booking page**.
4. Copy the link from the address bar, or use the **Share** option.
5. Send it by text or email, or put it in your bio.

Clients pick a time and it lands on your calendar automatically.
