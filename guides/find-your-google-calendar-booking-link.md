Set up a booking page but can't remember where the link went? Here are two quick ways to find it in Google Calendar.

## Option 1: From your calendar

1. Click the **waffle** (Google apps menu) at the top and choose **Calendar**.
2. Find the long **blue bar** on your calendar and click it. This is your appointment schedule.
3. Click **Copy link**, or **Preview** to see what clients will see.

You can also edit any of your appointment details from here.

## Option 2: From Booking pages

1. In Calendar, click **Booking pages** in the left column.
2. Choose the one you want.
3. Click **Copy link** or **Preview link**.

Send that link to your client and they pick a time that works for them.
