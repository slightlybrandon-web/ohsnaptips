Safety comes first at KW Memorial. This guide covers the **Red File**, the procedure that helps you stay safe when you are out showing homes.

## Find the safety documents

1. Open the **New Agent Drive** Dropbox link provided to you. It holds the files you need as a new agent.
2. Open the **Safety Procedures** folder.
3. You will see two files: the **Red File** and the **Safety Procedure Policy**.

## How the Red File works

If you are ever in a situation that you feel puts you in danger:

1. **Call the office.** Whoever answers will know what to do.
2. Ask for **the Red File** for the property address you are at.
3. **Give the property address.** That lets the person on the line call 911 right away and send first responders to you.

> Take time to learn this procedure. If you are ever the person on the other end of the phone, you can help save a life.
