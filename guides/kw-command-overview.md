Command is your real estate partner. Here is how to get set up and what each applet does.

## First: connect e-signatures

1. Go to **agent.kw.com** and log into Command.
2. Click your name in the top right corner and select **Settings**.
3. Find **DocuSign** and **Dotloop**. If they say **Connected**, you are done.
4. If not, click **Connect** and log in with the credentials from **UpTech Pro**.

## Set up your marketing profile

1. In the left column, click **Connect Settings**, open the drop-down and choose **Marketing Profile**.
2. Add your photos, and a team logo or personal logo.
3. Correct any information. The profile is **client-facing**, and newsletters and SmartPlans pull from it.
4. Add any military status or affiliation and your brokerage information.
5. Add the **legal footer text** provided for compliance, and your **IABS** if it isn't there (find it in HAR).
6. Add your social channels and click **Save**.

> Command remembers better than you do, so the more information you give it, the more it does for you.

## The applets at a glance

- **Home:** goals, reports and everything at a glimpse.
- **Contacts:** your database and CRM. Upload all your contacts and add birthdays, extra addresses, spouses and more.
- **Tasks:** your reminder and check-off list. Click **Create New Task** to add one.
- **SmartPlans:** your automated assistant. Sends texts, emails and quarterly updates to anyone you assign. Use pre-made plans or create your own.
- **Referrals:** connect with other KW agents. Click the map, choose a city, filter by criteria (such as languages, military or production level) and send your referral.
- **Opportunities:** where you start and end transactions, with all documents and a checklist in one place. Choose from listings, buyers or leases.
- **Campaigns:** newsletters, paid ads, emails, direct mailers and social posts in one place.
- **Reports:** goals, lead routing, emails, text messages and other metrics.
- **Designs:** email signatures, newsletters, listing presentations, open house flyers and more.
- **Listings:** sync your MLS so all your listings are in one view.
- **Consumer:** edit your website. Your KW app and website are one and the same. Add pages, open house landing pages and guide builders.

Your site lives at *yourname*.kw.com. It is customizable to an extent, with IDX search front and center, featured listings and resources for clients.

To learn more, use the Command resource links from your assessment or ask your Market Center's Command support team.
