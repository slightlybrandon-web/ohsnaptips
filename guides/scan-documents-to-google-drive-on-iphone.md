Use the Files app on your iPhone, connected to Google Drive, to scan any document and store it safely in your Drive account. You need the **Google Drive app** downloaded on your iPhone.

## Turn on Google Drive in Files

1. Open the **Files** app.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Turn on **Google Drive**.
4. Tap the **Drive** folder.

## Scan your document

1. Tap the **three dots** at the top right corner.
2. Select **Scan Documents**.
3. **Hold your document up** and let the app take the photo and scan it for you automatically.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Rename, find and send it

You can rename the file if you'd like. It's now safely stored in your Google Drive account.

- Find, edit and send the file through the **Files** app on your iPhone.
- Or open Google Drive online in your browser.
