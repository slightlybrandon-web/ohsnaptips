Linktree puts your digital portfolio and profile in one central place, like a digital business card for the web.

## Create your account

1. Go to **linktr.ee** and click **Get Started for Free**.
2. Pick a **username**. This becomes your URL, so choose something easy to remember.
3. Enter your **email** and choose a **password**.
4. **Verify your email** when Linktree sends the message.
5. Enter your name or business name, choose your **industry** and an optional subcategory.
6. Choose the **free** account. It is all you need to get started.

## Add your links

1. Log in and click **Add New Link**.
2. Title the link and paste the URL below it.
3. Repeat for as many links as you want, and drag the tiles into any order.
4. Tap **Explore** for more features, such as adding your own contact card so customers can save your information on the fly.

## Customize it

1. Open **Appearance** to style your profile card. There are many free options and paid upgrades.
2. Set the **profile title**. Best practice is your business name or your own name, depending on your brand.
3. Add a **bio** and **profile photo** if you'd like.

## Add social icons

1. Open **Settings** and select **Social Icons** in the left column.
2. Choose a social channel, enter its URL and click **Add to Linktree**.
3. Choose whether the icons show at the **top or bottom** of your page, and add as many as you like.

## Share it

1. Click **Share** in the top right and choose **Copy Linktree Link**.
2. Open a new tab and paste the link to see your finished page in full screen.

Share that link on all your favorite accounts.
