Your marketing profile feeds your SmartPlans, campaigns, consumer app and more, so keep it accurate.

## Open it

1. Log into **agent.kw.com** with your Command credentials.
2. Click your **name** on the right side and select **Settings**.
3. In the left column, click **Connect Settings**, then **Marketing Profile**.

## Fill it out

1. **Photo:** under **My Photo**, click **Upload** and choose your photo. For best resolution use a web-ready **360 x 360** image.
2. **Team or personal logo:** upload a **360 x 360** logo the same way. If you aren't part of a team, use your own logo.
3. **Details:** make sure everything is accurate and up to date. Clients and other KW agents will find it.
4. **Military:** if you are affiliated with the military, choose it in the drop-down. It shows other military-affiliated agents in the **Referrals** finder.
5. **Bio:** make sure it reflects your real estate business. If you need one, you can get a bio written on Fiverr for as little as five dollars.
6. **Contact info:** add your best phone number, office phone, fax if needed, email and website.

> Pro tip: put **https://www.** at the start of your website address so the link works.

7. **Brokerage:** check it is accurate. It is usually filled in for you.
8. **Legal footer:** paste the legal footer text from the video description or your assessment.
9. **Compliance:** if you are part of HAR, add your links. If not, take a screenshot of your **IABS** and **CPN**, store it in a Drive folder and paste that link.
10. **Branding:** add the social platforms you regularly use.
11. Click **Save**.

## Make sure your site is branded to you

Check that the **toggle** on the right side is **green**. If it is grayed out, turn it back on, acknowledge and continue. That way your agent site is branded to you.
