The DiSC personality assessment helps you learn how you interact with other people and how they interact with you, so you can build better working relationships.

## Steps

1. Open the **New Agent Drive**.
2. Click **DiSC Instructions**, then open the **DiSC Instruction PDF**.
3. Follow the steps in the guide to take the assessment. Set aside **20 to 25 minutes**.
4. When you finish, contact **Agent Services** to get your DiSC profile information.
5. Partner with a **leadership member** to learn more about how to use it.
