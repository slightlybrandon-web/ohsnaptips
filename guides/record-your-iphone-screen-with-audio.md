Most iPhone screen recordings come out silent because the microphone is off by default. Turn it on first and your voice is captured too.

## Record with audio

1. Open **Control Center** (swipe down from the top-right corner).
2. **Press and hold** the Record button (the circle inside a circle).
3. Tap **Microphone** so it turns red.
4. Tap **Start Recording** and wait for the countdown.
5. Tap the **red clock** or the Record button to stop.

Your video saves to the **Photos** app.

> **Pro tip:** Don't see the Record button? In Control Center, tap on the empty space until **Add a Control** appears, then add **Screen Recording**.

Use it for quick tutorials, client walkthroughs, or showing someone how to do something on their phone.
