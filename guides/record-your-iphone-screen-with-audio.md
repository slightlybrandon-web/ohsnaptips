Want to record your screen and your voice at the same time? Turn the microphone on before you start and you get both.

## Record with audio

1. Swipe down to open **Control Center**.
2. Find the **Screen Recording** button.
3. **Press and hold** it so more options pop up.
4. Turn your **Microphone** on.
5. Tap **Start Recording** and wait for the three-second countdown.

Now your screen is recorded with sound.

> **Pro tip:** Don't see the Screen Recording button? Place your finger anywhere in the empty space in Control Center until the controls start to wiggle, then tap **Add a Control**. Type **screen recorder** in the search box and choose the option you want.
