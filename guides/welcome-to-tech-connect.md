Welcome to Tech Connect. This guide walks through the accounts you will find in the **UpTech Pro paperwork** that was emailed to you. UpTech Pro is the company that sets up all of your accounts.

> Save the PDF in an easily findable place. It has all of your logins.

## Your accounts

- **MyKW** (mykw.kw.com): the front page of the Keller Williams internet and your go-to resource for everything.
- **Command:** your real estate partner. Your MyKW login also signs you into Command, so get to know it.
- **KW email:** your kw.com email is hosted by Gmail. Go to gmail.com and sign in with the email address and password provided.
- **E-signature apps:** you have **Dotloop** and **DocuSign**. Log in and create your accounts so you have access to both.
- **Command website:** hosted through Command at *yourname*.kw.com. Edit it from your Command page.
- **Circlepix:** your core listing machine. It posts blogs and marketplace listings to social media for you. Log in with the provided details and add your Facebook, Twitter or YouTube page.
- **KW PowerLister Deluxe:** for when you need a listing presentation on the fly. A quick template gets you a polished presentation in a few minutes.
- **Michael Lewis Marketing Suite:** free email signatures and customizable designs for business cards, flyers and other templates. Your login is provided in the paperwork.
