Your KW email address is hosted by Gmail. Here is how to log in, use a few of the best features and keep your signature compliant.

## Sign in

1. Go to **gmail.com**.
2. If you are already signed into another Gmail account, click your **profile picture** in the top right and choose **Add another account**.
3. Enter your KW email address and password. Both are on your **UpTech Pro sheet**.

## Handy Google apps

Click the **grid icon** in the top right corner to see your apps.

- **Google Drive:** like a garage for your digital files online. Store files, music, movies and more.
- **Docs, Sheets and Slides:** the Google versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint. They open and save Microsoft files too.
- **Calendar:** includes an **appointment scheduler**, a feature KWRI covers the cost of.

## Create an appointment scheduler

1. In **Calendar**, click **Create** in the top left and choose **Appointment schedule**.
2. Name it (for example, "House Consultations").
3. Choose your times and availability in the weekly schedule, so clients can only book the times you give them. Click **Next**.
4. Add a short description so clients know what to do.
5. Add any extra information you need from them, such as phone number, email or address, and any reminders.
6. Click **Save**, then **copy the link** and share it with clients.

You and the client are both notified when someone books.

## Add the email disclaimer to your signature

1. Click the **Settings** gear and choose **See all settings**.
2. Find **Signature** and select the signature you want to edit.
3. Space down one or two lines and **paste the email disclaimer** that was provided to you.
4. Repeat for **every** signature you have, so all of your accounts stay compliant.
5. Scroll to the bottom and click **Save**.
